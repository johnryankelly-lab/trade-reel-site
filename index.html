What we make

Coverage built for the week after the show.

  1. 01

    Event films & recaps

    Daily highlights, sizzle reels and the final recap, cut to the show's pace and delivered while attendees are still talking about it.

  2. 02

    Executive & customer interviews

    Two-light booth interviews with the people your buyers want to hear from. Presidents, engineers, longtime customers.

  3. 03

    Same-day social

    Vertical edits, captions and stills posted from the floor, for your channels or the show's official accounts.

  4. 04

    Product launches & reveals

    The unveil, the walkaround and the reaction, planned in advance so the moment is captured from every angle once.

  5. 05

    Keynote & stage coverage

    Multi-camera session capture with clean audio, speaker spotlights and audience cutaways, sponsor-ready on delivery.

  6. 06

    Media kits & content libraries

    Every clip, still and interview organized and handed off ready for your agency, your press page or next year's prospectus.

Add-ons: livestream and hybrid broadcast, drone (venue permitting), podcast and audio interviews, subtitles and translation, scriptwriting and teleprompter, 2D/3D motion graphics.

How a show runs

Before. During. Long after.

  1. Weeks out

    Plan the story

    Pre-show call, booth schedule, interview list, launch timing and where each deliverable is headed.

  2. On the floor

    Capture and cut

    Crew on site from move-in. Social edits and stills go out the same day; rough cuts overnight.

  3. After the show

    Put it to work

    Final films, interview library and media kit, organized for sales, recruiting, members and press.
